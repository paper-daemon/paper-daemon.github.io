FAQ

Where do I enter a Muse invite code? In Muse, Settings > General > Redeem invite code — within 48 hours of creating your account.

What does a code give me? 1 billion Muse tokens for you, and 1 billion for the sharer. The tokens are additional tokens with no expiry.

Is this page official? No — this is an unofficial community page, not affiliated with Meta or Muse. Codes are only ever redeemed inside the Muse app; never enter your Muse password anywhere else.
