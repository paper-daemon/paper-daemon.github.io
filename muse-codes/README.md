## How Muse invite codes work

- Join Muse, then redeem a code in **Settings → General → Redeem invite code** within **48 hours** of signing up.
- You receive **1 billion Muse tokens** — and so does the person who shared the code.
- The bonus tokens are additional tokens and **never expire**.
