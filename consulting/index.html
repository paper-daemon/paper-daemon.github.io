AI Implementation Consulting

A one-time consulting package for small companies and teams: it designs your AI setup before anything is built. The focus is not automation for its own sake. The focus is work on the PC that keeps running — scheduled, logged, and checked — including the hours when nobody is watching it.

By Riona Amase. The method is the one used in my own operation, where AI agents, content publishing, and site maintenance run on a continuing basis.

¥300,000 one-time (approx. USD 2,000 — reference only, billing in JPY)
Fixed scope as listed below. Build work and ongoing operation are not included.

What you receive

  1. Workflow audit — A written map of your recurring work (content, social media, store operations, reporting, customer replies) and the specific points where it stalls.
  2. Implementation design — For each task: where AI belongs, where it does not, tool selection, the workflow (input, processing, human check, output), staged access design, running-cost estimate, and how to roll back if something fails.
  3. Prioritized roadmap — Build order in three phases. Each item lists expected effect, difficulty, prerequisites, and a time estimate. Tasks that should stay manual are named explicitly.
  4. Build-ready spec for the first workflow — The top-priority workflow specified in enough detail to build from: inputs, outputs, check points, failure handling, logging.
  5. Handover notes — Run rules, what to check weekly, and how work moves into monthly operations if you want it run for you.

How it works

  • Week 0: You complete a written intake form and share materials (documents, screenshots, exports). No account access is needed.
  • Week 1: Audit delivered, with written Q&A.
  • Week 2: Design, roadmap, and the first-workflow spec delivered.
  • Week 3: Your review, one revision round, final delivery.

All communication is text-based and asynchronous. There are no calls or meetings in this engagement — everything is written, which keeps a full record of decisions.

Access and security

  • I never ask for passwords or authentication codes, and I do not take custody of credentials.
  • If anything needs to be viewed together, only the platform's official sharing and permission features are used, with the smallest scope that works.
  • Payments, contracts, pricing changes, and refunds stay with you. The design can cover those workflows; executing them is not part of this service.
  • Work performed and materials received are logged, so you can check what was touched and when.

Who this is for

  • Small companies whose AI, social media, or e-commerce operations keep stalling.
  • Teams that set up AI tools but have nobody whose job it is to keep them running.
  • Businesses whose “automation” turned out to be a folder of scripts nobody maintains.
