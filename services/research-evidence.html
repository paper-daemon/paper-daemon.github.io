What comes back.

Source log

Source title, URL, access date, topic or claim, evidence note, verification state, and relevant reuse notes.

Evidence map

Important claims are tied to supporting sources. Conflicts and inference are separated instead of blended into a confident paragraph.

Handoff summary

Key findings, comparison table where useful, strongest sources, unresolved items, caveats, and the next verification step.

WORKED EXAMPLE

A simple evidence workflow.

QUESTION → SOURCES → CLAIMS → HANDOFF

Keep the chain visible.

Example claim: “Product A supports feature X in Japan.” A good handoff records the official product page, its publication or access date, whether the support is general or region-specific, and any conflicting documentation. If the evidence does not settle the claim, the result stays marked unresolved.
